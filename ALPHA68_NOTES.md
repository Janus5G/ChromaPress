# ChromaPress v1 Alpha 68 — Part 4 Complete

Alpha 68 completes the planned Part 4 sequence with TPM-backed recovery-key protection policy. Target metadata must verify TPM2 tooling plus a TSS2 stack before a policy intent can be staged. Physical TPM presence, ownership, PCR policy, key generation and sealing remain apply/runtime checks. TPM is not a biometric authenticator and does not prove platform-authenticator capability.

This release includes Alpha 65–68 together in one delivery and one consolidated Part 4 test package. No intermediate Alpha 65/66/67 release ZIPs are required.
